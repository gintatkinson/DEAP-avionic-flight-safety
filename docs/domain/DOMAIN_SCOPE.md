## 1. Scope & Domain Boundary

The `DEAP-avionic-flight-safety` repository serves as the Parent Domain Specification Template (Tier 1) for airborne system flight safety, system safety assessments, software design assurance, and hardware development assurance.

### Normative Standards Baseline:
- **RTCA DO-178C / EUROCAE ED-12C**: Software Considerations in Airborne Systems and Equipment Certification (DAL A through DAL E).
- **RTCA DO-254 / EUROCAE ED-80**: Design Assurance Guidance for Airborne Electronic Hardware.
- **SAE ARP4754A / EUROCAE ED-79A**: Guidelines for Development of Civil Aircraft and Systems.
- **SAE ARP4761**: Guidelines and Methods for Conducting the Safety Assessment Process on Civil Airborne Systems and Equipment (FHA, PSSA, SSA, CCA).
- **MIL-STD-882E**: Department of Defense Standard Practice for System Safety (Tasks 101–107, 201–207, Risk Assessment Matrix).
